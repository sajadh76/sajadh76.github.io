About

I'm a doctoral researcher in Information Systems at the University of Geneva. Previously, I was a machine learning research intern at Nokia Bell Labs, across its Belgium and France offices. I received my MSc in Telecommunications Engineering from Politecnico di Milano.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and cybersecurity, with a focus on privacy-preserving machine learning and federated learning. Recently, I have also begun exploring federated foundation models, their security and optimization.
